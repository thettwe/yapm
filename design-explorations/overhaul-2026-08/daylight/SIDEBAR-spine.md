# SIDEBAR — "-spine" · THE CYCLE SPINE

**Concept.** Every tracker's sidebar is a taxonomy — a tree of places. This one is state: the
current cycle is the hero object, drawn in the frame itself, and the views become lenses on it.
A PM who never clicks still knows the day, the arc, what shipped, and what needs attention.

**Elements, annotated:**
- Workspace row + `⌘K` keycap — static chrome, one row (search collapsed into it). LIVE: no.
- **Cycle card** — "Cycle 2 · ends Fri", a 14-tick day band (8 elapsed terracotta-soft, today
  full terracotta, 5 ahead faint), "Day 9 of 14", then plain words: 8 shipped / 3 in review /
  1 needs attention. Entirely LIVE — the one object no other tracker's sidebar can render.
- **Lenses** (List · Board · Delivery · Retro) live *inside* the card below a hairline — the
  containment says "ways of looking at Cycle 2". Static labels, keycap hints 1–4; active row
  carries the terracotta rail.
- **Outside the cycle**: Triage (LIVE "2 new" intake), Backlog (LIVE 34), Projects (LIVE
  "3 active"), Roadmap (static). Named for the relationship, not as a tree.
- **Personal strip** (bottom): Inbox 3 unread, My Issues 4 open — both LIVE, own-work only.
- **Team switcher**: E ringed + "Engineering", D/P as quiet marks — static, one row, rare action.

**Deleted from the Linear IA:** the search field row and Search nav item (→ ⌘K), the top
Inbox/My Issues block (→ bottom strip), the expanded teams tree (→ marks), and the "Cycles"
leaf — the cycle isn't a place you visit, it *is* the sidebar.
