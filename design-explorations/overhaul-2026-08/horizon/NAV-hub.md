# horizon/hub — the nav model

**The bet:** nav chrome nearly disappears. Band 1 (48px, byte-identical on every page) carries
only: Acme / Engineering as a quiet workspace/team menu, **Home** as the single named
destination, then the globals — a wide "Search or jump to… ⌘K" pill (the believed primary
switcher), the attention badge (dot + 1), Inbox (3), the user chip. Nothing in it ever adapts.

**Home is the hub.** Every surface is reached three ways: from a Home section (each section IS
the doorway — Needs Attention rows open their issue/Board/Triage, Ship Cadence's header opens
"Delivery in full ›", the onward footer covers Issues/Delivery/Retro/Roadmap), from ⌘K, or from
the masthead's local context (Issues offers Board as a sibling toggle; every non-Home masthead
opens with a quiet "← Home").

**What died from the rail, and where it went:** the TEAMS tree → the workspace/team menu in
band 1; List/Board leaves → the masthead sibling toggle; Delivery/Retros/Projects/Roadmap/
Triage leaves → Home's sections and onward footer, plus ⌘K; Inbox → band 1; My Issues → ⌘K
only (the one real loss — it has no drawn doorway); Connected → the statusline's "Synced";
the spine's cycle card → the Home hero itself, where the maintainer said it belongs.

**Orientation discipline:** anywhere in the app you see three fixed anchors — Home at top-left,
your page's own masthead naming where you are, and the statusline speaking the cycle. Any page
to any page is two interactions: ← Home, then a section; or ⌘K, then type.
