# PLAY — warmth: identity in the moments

**The idea.** A tracker's personality lives in the states nobody budgets for. This play designs
six of them as one system — a drawn-mark language (1.6px round-cap strokes, the exact DNA of the
status glyphs and reality tracks), plain reassuring words, and a single piece of print-shop
warmth (the terracotta LIVE stamp) rationed to the one moment that earns it — then spends the
whole budget on the retro room, the one surface allowed to be 10% more human: paper notes with
dog-ears and sub-degree rotation on a `--bg-sidebar` tabletop, phase stepper in the cycle
day-band language, terracotta dot-votes, and the anonymity guarantee written on the surface in
mono ("cards are anonymous by design — there is no author column") because the schema actually
makes it true. The retro shows the **vote** phase deliberately: per the retrospective spec it is
the only moment where revealed anonymous cards, the AI draft (born at the reveal, reading the
work graph only — stated on-surface) and live dots truthfully coexist.

**Why it is yapm's own.** Every warm move restates a hard guarantee — local-first sync, the
storage-level no-author-column, the AI's table allowlist, the phase state machine ("writing
closed when the cards were revealed") — warmth as the voice of the architecture, not decoration.

**Graduate:** the phrase set for empty/offline/no-results states; the anonymity line on the retro;
the draft band's subordinate placement + "reads the work graph only" copy; the drawn-mark stroke
spec. **Stay a sketch:** the LIVE stamp (charming once, policy risk at scale), note rotation and
dog-ears, the seedling/sun illustrations pending a fuller icon audit.
