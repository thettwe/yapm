CONSTRUCTION every mark is drawn once, on the same 20 grid, with the same pen
The loopa 7-radius circle on a 20 grid; status is how much of it has been drawn
The tickone unit of weight; count says how much, color says how loud
The slasha 70° stroke; doubled it is the divergence mark, single it cancels a loop
Rules of the language dots are facts, rings are promises, arcs are progress, ticks are weight, the slash is the difference between them. Nothing is filled unless it is true; nothing points, blinks, or spins.
