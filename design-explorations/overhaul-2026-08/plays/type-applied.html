THE PACE

About 3 releases a week reach production now — it was 1 a week in February. Across those six months, 48 releases reached customers; two were rolled back within the hour, and three were replaced by a newer release before anyone saw them. The cadence didn’t come from working weekends. It came from smaller changes: the typical change now merges in 46 hours, half of what it took in the spring.
